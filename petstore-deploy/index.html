Unique Insight: The 10-Order Ceiling

The store/order endpoint constrains orderId to the range 1–10. This means the entire order system can only track 10 concurrent orders, creating a hard bottleneck. With 236 available pets and 54 pending, this constraint makes the order pipeline practically unusable at scale.
